State meanings under the amended proposal:
S: all necessary elements established by eligible evidence.
P: at least one necessary element established, another unresolved, and no established necessary-element contradiction.
U: at least one necessary element contradicted by eligible evidence.
I: neither a contradiction nor a sufficient substantive positive element is established.

The original S rule required direct contemporaneous evidence. The proposed reconstructive admission of sufficiently grounded later evidence is an amendment, not a correction that can be applied without author adoption. The original P/U temporal eligibility and several operational requirements were less explicit. Where that ambiguity prevents a unique unchanged-policy recoding, this ledger does not invent one.

Change tags used below

E: correction or reconsideration of a source-to-condition inference.
D: amendment or clarification of the operational definition/necessary elements, including the threshold that separates P from I.
B: explicit change or narrowing of actor, options, commitment point or execution boundary.
A: amendment to evidence admission, especially allowing later reconstruction to establish S while tracking original-record availability separately.
G: aggregation change. G is none for every row in this proposed reanalysis.

Multiple tags can apply. An unchanged letter can conceal a changed proposition or rationale. No historical source added after the frozen cutoff is used here.
