Additional reassessment of three ZG710 conditions

Provisional author review, separate from the historical baseline and unchanged Stage 2 comparison. Preserve the original classification-to-destruction boundary and its informed-challenge claim. The adverse findings remain evidence; their inference to a necessary failure remains unresolved.

Released rules: P means “some required elements are present and a material gap remains”; U means “available evidence contradicts the stage or shows the condition was absent”; I means “the packet lacks enough evidence to decide.” Rejecting a U inference does not automatically establish I or P. S still requires direct contemporaneous evidence; U has no symmetrical gate. Later evidence supporting P needs explicit eligibility adjudication under the incompletely specified policy.

Amendments kept separate

D: changing necessary capacity, imposing action-plus-evaluation or mandatory challenge; B/D: replacing informed challenge with simple withholding; B: substituting collective-role evidence for individual capacity; A: admitting later reconstruction as S. None is adopted here. Resolving ambiguity is an explicit interpretive judgment; adopting a new rule or eligibility policy must be recorded as an amendment.
