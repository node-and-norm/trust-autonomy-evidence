## Open limits and future studies

Phase closure does not close the five inherited base-audit exceptions or the two policy-crosswalk exceptions. Independent assessment, incomplete literature coverage, direct-query source limits, untested contemporary transfer, external venue status, legal/standards coverage and prospective policy application remain recorded limitations. Earlier frozen gate files retain their original scope and statuses.

Potential future studies are separately authorized and versioned:

1. Independent reconstruction or a clean-room implementation, preserving disagreements and assessor provenance.
2. One contemporary learned or agentic case with timestamps adequate to test intervention and execution propagation.
3. A prospective or realistically simulated institutional decision in which a failed condition can stop or alter execution.
4. A prespecified human/AI comparison that can measure time and quality benefits without reusing an exposed baseline.
5. Further literature recovery, including 980 population outcomes, the 177-record backward-reference stratum, authenticated Scopus/Web of Science and disciplinary-interface searches.
6. New frozen case families or Jev experiments, with fresh protocols before observing outputs.

Independent reliability, construct validity, prevalence, field validity, contemporary-system transfer, institutional effectiveness, source truth, universal originality, safety and harm prevention remain unestablished. The separate CEC infrastructure has its own work and claim boundaries; this closeout transfers no TAE validation claim to it.

## Versioning rule

A release may change the model, evidence requirements, or protocol only when the changelog identifies the affected proposition, supporting evidence, migration effect, and remaining uncertainty. Historical states are preserved; later corrections and reassessments identify their evidence and interpretation dependencies explicitly.
