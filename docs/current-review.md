## Questions for a substantive review

The following questions follow the [research limits](../LIMITATIONS.md) and [current maturity record](../RESEARCH_STATUS.md#current-maturity).

1. Does each condition match the practical-control claim it is intended to assess?
2. Does each source support the judgment attributed to it?
3. Are missing records, conflicting accounts and retained alternatives treated consistently?
4. Does the case finding follow from the declared aggregation rule?
5. What additional evidence would change a judgment or support transfer to a contemporary AI system?
