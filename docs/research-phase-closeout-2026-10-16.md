## Completed scope

- A documentary method linking six necessary event conditions to an explicit
  categorical result, with traceable evidence and missingness rules.
- Three purposively selected historical packets, preserved released assessments,
  a separately identified limited correction, and the approved additional ZG710
  reassessment. The additional result is conditional across 64 retained assignments.
- A public 46-page revised preprint, editable source archive, public approval
  statement, correction record, supplement, and content hashes.
- Offline reproduction of historical and corrected results, all 4,096 legal
  six-condition rows, and the priority of any necessary-condition failure.
- Preserved first live Jev results, diagnostics, review-assistance pilot, and
  separate collaborative author review. Core TAE validation remains deterministic
  and credential-free.

The narrow reassessment preserves the original conditions, case boundaries,
aggregation rule, and strict contemporaneous threshold for Supported. It does not
adopt the broader Stage 3 proposals. Historical release bytes and sealed synthetic
oracles remain unchanged.

## Unresolved matters retained at closure

These remain limitations and possible future studies, not accomplishments of the
closed phase:

- Independent application, assessor agreement, construct validity, field effects,
  and transfer to contemporary learned or agentic systems.
- The six retained P/I coding alternatives and the evidentiary validity of the
  supplied state sets; invariant aggregation does not settle those choices.
- Incomplete literature retrieval and authenticated-database coverage, legal and
  standards coverage, and venue-specific requirements.
- All 1,079 original Jev disagreement/invalid-determination adjudications remain
  UNRESOLVED. The 24-item assisted review does not close those records.
- The omitted standardized human comparison; human time savings and quality
  improvement remain unmeasured.

No arXiv moderation outcome, independent reliability, safety benefit, institutional
effectiveness, exhaustive originality, or legal sufficiency is claimed.

## Boundary for a next research phase

An arXiv announcement alone does not start a new research phase. It changes the
publication record. A new study requires a chosen question, bounded claim,
prospective protocol, frozen inputs, acceptance criteria, and a stop condition.
Independent replication or a contemporary trace-based case could be separate
future phases if the author chooses to pursue them. CEC retains its own work,
evidence, and claims.

Maintenance after closure is limited to verified publication metadata, genuine
errata, dependency/security needs, or an explicitly authorized new study. New
observations cannot silently revise the historical findings or reopen frozen
Jev protocols.
