## Question and contribution

A person appears in the workflow. What evidence shows they could understand, intervene and affect the action?

A reproducible documentary assessment method, demonstrated on three selected historical cases. The approved conditional reassessment leaves all three Unresolved.

## Findings to date

The historical baseline produced two Fail findings and one Unresolved finding. A limited correction changes one result; the additional ZG710 reassessment yields three Unresolved case findings across all 64 retained joint assignments. These comparisons remain separately preserved.

## Current status and why

The defined methods demonstration and initial Jev phase closed on 16 October 2026 after publication-package reconciliation. Their completed scope, corrections and open matters were preserved. Publication administration and genuine errata can continue without reopening the phase.

## Limits and next evidence

One researcher assessed three purposively selected historical cases. Independent reliability, construct validity, transfer to contemporary AI systems and effects on human review remain unestablished. All 1,079 original Jev adjudications remain unresolved.

An independently scoped replication or contemporary trace-based study needs a chosen question, frozen inputs, prospective protocol, acceptance criteria and stopping condition.
