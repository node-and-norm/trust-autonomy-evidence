# Limits on interpretation

The author-created fixtures, rules and oracle share a design source. Their categorical signals already summarize evidence, and question criteria expose the mapping explicitly. High agreement would establish successful execution of a small synthetic classification contract. It would provide no evidence of independent reliability, source reconstruction, field validity, institutional effectiveness, legal sufficiency, improved outcomes or overall practical human control.

The 252 determinations are clustered within 12 deliberately constructed cases. They do not represent a deployment population. Model probabilities and confidence are retained as reported, without asserting that they are calibrated for TAE. The uniform mock outputs are fabricated test data and must never be cited as Jev findings.

The model sees the complete normalized state. Cross-field influence and nuisance sensitivity remain possible despite instructions. Title invariance is enforced by omission. Prespecified mutations support inspection of sensitivity and invariance, but one before/after pair cannot distinguish stochastic variation from a causal response. Additional repetitions require a separately recorded protocol and budget.

The SDK and model pin constrain reproducibility; hosted service behavior, backend changes and transport dependencies can still change. A resolved model identifier is provider-reported provenance, not an independently verified model-weight digest. HTTP response hashes cover bytes exposed by the client after HTTP content decoding, not encrypted network traffic. Request hashes cover saved JSON payloads; SDK serialization can use different whitespace.

External API credentials are required for live execution. Missing credentials, transport failures and invalid output schemas are visible blocked/incomplete runs. They cannot be counted as agreement, indeterminate evidence, or successful validation. The experiment has no permission to revise the sealed oracle or released TAE findings.
